These lists are associated with body areas. Selecting a specific muscle highlights that anatomy and shows the common issues for its area.

## Neck

- **Position-related neck pain**: Sleeping awkwardly or spending a long time at a desk can contribute to neck pain.
  Sources: [NHS: Neck pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/neck-pain-and-stiff-neck/).
- **Pinched nerve**: Pressure on a nerve is one possible cause of neck pain.
  Sources: [NHS: Neck pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/neck-pain-and-stiff-neck/).

## Shoulder

- **Rotator cuff tear or tendon irritation**: Damage or irritation affecting the tendons that help lift and rotate your arm.
  Sources: [AAOS: Rotator cuff tears](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/rotator-cuff-tears/); [NHS: Tendonitis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tendonitis/).
- **Shoulder impingement**: A shoulder tendon rubbing against nearby tissue or bone, which can hurt when you raise your arm.
  Sources: [NHS: Shoulder impingement](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/shoulder-impingement-syndrome/).
- **Frozen shoulder**: Pain and stiffness that make the shoulder difficult to move.
  Sources: [NHS: Frozen shoulder](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/frozen-shoulder/).
- **Shoulder bursitis**: Inflamed cushioning sacs near a joint can cause tenderness, swelling and pain with movement.
  Sources: [NHS: Bursitis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/bursitis/).

## Upper Back

- **Muscle strain**: An overstretched or torn muscle can cause soreness, weakness or swelling.
  Sources: [NHS: Sprains and strains](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sprains-and-strains/).

## Lower Back

- **Muscle strain**: An overstretched or torn muscle can cause soreness, weakness or swelling.

## Arm

- **Muscle strain**: An overstretched or torn muscle can cause soreness, weakness or swelling.
  Sources: [NHS: Sprains and strains](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sprains-and-strains/).
- **Tendon irritation (tendonitis)**: Inflammation of tissue connecting muscle to bone can make movement painful or stiff.

## Elbow

## Wrist/Hand

- **Joint sprain**: Stretched or torn ligaments around a joint can cause pain, swelling or bruising.
  Sources: [NHS: Sprains and strains](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sprains-and-strains/).
- **Carpal tunnel syndrome**: Pressure on a nerve in the wrist can cause tingling, numbness and pain in the hand.
  Sources: [NHS: Carpal tunnel syndrome](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/carpal-tunnel-syndrome/).

## Hip
