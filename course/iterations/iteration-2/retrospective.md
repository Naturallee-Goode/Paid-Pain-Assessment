# Iteration 2 Retrospective

## Keep

- Continue planning ahead and creating clear backlog items so everyone knows what needs to be done.
- Continue regular sponsor meetings to keep the team aligned with the project goals and priorities.

## Problem

- The sponsor was sometimes unavailable, which delayed feedback and decisions needed to move work forward.
- Too many PRs were waiting for review, with too few team members reviewing each other's work on time. This created a backlog and delayed merging changes.

## Change

- Talk with the sponsor and agree on regular meeting times that work for both the sponsor and the team.
- Coordinate peer reviews so each PR has a reviewer and an agreed review time. Share the review workload across the team to prevent another backlog.
